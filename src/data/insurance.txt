Insurance Claim Policy

This policy applies to all customers who purchase a device protection plan.
Coverage begins on the date the policy is activated and continues while the account remains active.

Customers can submit an accidental damage claim within 30 days of the incident.
Claims filed after 30 days are generally not eligible unless an exception is approved.

The device must be covered under an active policy.
A device is covered only when the policy is valid and the premium has been paid.

The customer must provide proof of purchase.
Accepted proof includes an invoice, receipt, order confirmation, or delivery email.
Without proof of purchase, the claim may be denied or delayed.

Claims for intentional damage are not covered.
Damage caused by misuse, neglect, or deliberate acts is excluded from reimbursement.

A claim can be rejected if the policy has expired.
Claims submitted after the policy expiration date are not eligible for coverage.

All claims must be reported through the official support portal or authorized service channel.
A claim number is issued after the application is reviewed.

The insurer may request photographs of the damaged device before approving any repair or replacement.
The customer must allow inspection or additional documentation if requested.

Repair eligibility depends on the age of the device and the type of damage reported.
If the device is not repairable, the insurer may offer replacement or cash settlement.

A claim is not covered if the damage was caused by water exposure, tampering, or unauthorized modifications.
Damage caused by unauthorized third-party repair attempts is excluded.

Policyholders are responsible for keeping contact information updated.
The insurer may contact the customer by email or phone to request missing documents.

If a claim is denied, the customer may request a written explanation of the decision.
The written explanation must be issued within 10 business days after the request is received.

Claims involving theft or lost items must be reported to law enforcement before the insurer reviews the case.
Police reports may be required as part of the review process.

Approved claims may include repair, replacement, or reimbursement for eligible expenses.
The insurer reserves the right to choose the service method or replacement option.

The customer must cooperate with the claims investigation process.
Failure to provide required information can result in a delayed or denied claim.

A claim for cosmetic damage may be considered only if the damage is covered under the policy terms.
Cosmetic issues unrelated to functionality are often excluded from reimbursement.

The policy does not cover routine wear and tear.
Normal aging, minor scratches, or expected usage degradation are not claimable events.

The service provider may require a diagnostic evaluation before approving a claim.
The cost of unsupported or unapproved diagnostics is the responsibility of the customer.

The insurer may approve partial reimbursement in cases where only a portion of the damage is covered.
Any partial approval must be documented in writing before payment is released.

This policy is subject to change with 30 days' written notice to active policyholders.
The latest policy version governs all claims submitted after the notice date.

Coverage may be suspended if the customer provides false information in a claim submission.
Fraudulent or misleading statements may void coverage and trigger legal review.

The policy covers accidental damage only when the event is sudden and unexpected.
Damage from gradual deterioration is excluded from coverage.

The insurer may request proof of ownership before issuing a replacement device.
Ownership confirmation may include a serial number, account record, or purchase receipt.

The customer must ensure the insured device is used within normal operating conditions.
Abuse, misuse, or unsafe handling may invalidate a claim.

A claim may be closed if required documentation is not submitted within 15 business days.
The insurer may reopen a claim only if new evidence is provided.

A claim may be approved for battery failure only when the condition is not caused by misuse or aftermarket alterations.
Battery-related issues caused by tampering are not covered.

The customer must retain original packaging and damaged parts when requested by the insurer.
Failure to preserve evidence can weaken a claim investigation.

The fund for replacement devices is limited to the current market value of the covered item.
The insurer may consider prior depreciation when calculating replacement value.

The claim review process may take up to 14 business days once all documents are submitted.
Incomplete submissions may extend the timeline beyond the standard review period.

A claim may be denied if the product is used for commercial purposes without proper coverage.
Commercial use is excluded unless explicitly listed in the policy.

The customer must notify the insurer immediately after discovering a covered incident.
Delayed reporting can reduce eligibility for reimbursement.

Policyholders may appeal a denied claim by submitting a written review request within 20 days.
The review request must include the reason for appeal and any additional evidence.

The insurer reserves the right to complete a final review before issuing benefits.
No verbal promise of coverage shall be binding unless approved in writing.

The policy includes a grace period for premium renewal based on the billing plan selected by the customer.
If the premium is unpaid, coverage may lapse and claims may be rejected.

This document is intended to summarize coverage terms and is not a substitute for the full legal policy wording.
In the event of a conflict, the formal contract controls.

All disputes must be addressed in a good-faith resolution process before litigation.
The insurer may offer settlement discussions as part of dispute resolution.

The customer acknowledges that the insurer may share claim information with approved service partners.
This information sharing is necessary to complete repairs or replacements.

The policy is valid only for devices listed on the active coverage account.
Devices added after the policy start date require separate review and approval.

Claims for accessories, peripherals, or software-only issues are usually excluded unless specifically covered.
Accessories purchased separately may not be included in reimbursement.

The insurer may require the device to be returned in a secure and working condition for inspection.
Shipping and handling charges incurred without prior approval may be denied.

The customer is responsible for safeguarding the device until the claim is resolved.
Loss or theft after the incident may not be covered if the device was not secured.

This policy may be canceled by the insurer for nonpayment, fraud, or serious policy violations.
Cancellation decisions are communicated in writing to the customer.

The insurer may update coverage terms and deductibles as part of yearly policy reviews.
Any changes are communicated through official customer channels and account notices.

This summary policy is intended for educational and demonstration purposes and may differ from the final contractual wording.
The final policy document governs any actual claim decision.
